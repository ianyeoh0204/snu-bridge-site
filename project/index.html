01 THE PROBLEM

English coursework still comes
with Korean administration.

A translated sentence can leave the most important questions unanswered. Eligibility may depend on degree level or a particular course. A required signature may be in a form, while dates are inside an image table.

Does this apply to me?

Degree, cohort, course registration, and exceptions matter. A missing condition should remain uncertain.

What exactly must I do?

Students need the complete sequence, submission route, forms, signatures, and deadline.

What supports the answer?

The original Korean evidence and unreadable material should remain visible.

The original aim still stands: monitor once, explain clearly, and alert the right student. The gaps are English-board coverage, understanding administrative requirements, unread images/forms, and repeated manual checking.

The intended guide explains applicability, every relevant deadline, required steps, forms, consequences, and open questions. Current official sources take priority over older examples. Missing information is flagged rather than filled in.

A concrete example: Thesis Research 430.960

Notice 58022 requires enrolled graduate students to complete both a Google Form and a signed confirmation submitted to Room 319. The source states the consequence of not submitting.

Scope: department academic notices, starting with ECE. Other department academic boards require their own verified configuration. Visa, settlement, and central university services are outside this project.
