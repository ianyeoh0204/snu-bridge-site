How SNU Bridge works

ECE academic notices are posted in Korean only. SNU Bridge reads each one every day and shows it in English, with what to do and by when.

–notices in English
–of paragraphs and table cells translated
–summaries pass the source check

Every morning at 08:17 (Korea time)

Runs on its own. Nobody has to press a button.

  1. CollectReads the ECE academic board and finds new or edited notices. Text only, following the site's robots.txt.
  2. Explain and translateAn AI model (Claude Opus 5.5) writes the short “At a glance” summary and translates the whole notice, tables included.
  3. CheckEvery Korean quote and date in a summary must appear in the notice. Every number, link and email must survive translation. Anything that fails is shown in Korean, never as wrong English.
  4. PublishThe site is rebuilt and every page is checked at phone and laptop width. If a run fails, a GitHub issue opens automatically.

What you do

  1. Pick your degreeThe notice list shows what applies to you, nearest deadline first.
  2. Open a notice“At a glance” first, then the full notice in English. Switch to Both or 한국어 to compare with the original.
  3. Still unsure?Copy a ready-made question into ChatGPT Edu. If it cannot answer, it tells you who to contact.

Not covered yet

  • Images and attached filesNot translated yet: the ECE site does not allow automated tools to read them. Each notice lists its files and links to them.
  • Other departmentsECE first. Each department board is a separate setting, added once it is checked.
  • Official statusThis is an AI translation, not the department's. The Korean original controls.
