How SNU Bridge works

ECE academic notices are posted in Korean only. SNU Bridge reads each one every day and shows it in English, with what to do and by when.

–notices in English
–of paragraphs and table cells translated

Every day, by itself

Nobody has to press a button. The notice list shows when the board was last checked.

  1. CollectReads the ECE academic board and finds new or edited notices, with their images and attached files (PDF, HWP, Word, Excel). One request per second; the files themselves are not kept or re-posted.
  2. Explain and translateAn AI model (Claude Opus 5.5) reads the text in images and files and writes the short “At a glance” summary from all of it. A second model (Claude Sonnet 5.5) translates the whole notice, tables and attachments included.
  3. CheckEvery Korean quote and date in a summary must appear in the notice or its files. Every number, link and email must survive translation; a part that fails is shown in Korean. A second AI pass then looks for meaning errors (same day or next day, who, conditions, “not”). None of this proves every sentence is right, so the Korean original stays one tap away.
  4. PublishThe site is rebuilt and every page is checked at phone and laptop width. If a run fails, a GitHub issue opens automatically.

What you do

  1. Pick your degreeThe notice list shows what applies to you, nearest deadline first. Subscribe once and the same deadlines appear in your phone calendar.
  2. Open a notice“At a glance” first, then the full notice in English. Switch to Both or 한국어 to compare with the original.
  3. Still unsure?Copy a ready-made question into ChatGPT Edu. If it cannot answer, it tells you who to contact.

Not covered yet

  • Some filesZIP bundles, password-protected files and very long documents are not translated in full; each notice says which.
  • Other departmentsECE only for now. Each department board needs its own address and reader; none has been added yet.
  • Official statusThis is an AI translation, not the department's. The Korean original controls.
